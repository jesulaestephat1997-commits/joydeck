Parenting Challenges

I was a 30-year old woman raising my 12-year-old daughter and 14-year-old son without any financial or emotional support. Parenting is instinctively based on our upbringing and, to a greater extent, our education. I was a high school dropout who learned how to code—that was my only claim to success. I admit I did not know how to parent my children, but it was my maternal instinct, love, and commitment to protecting them that became my guiding principles for parenting. When all else failed, love was my guiding light. I had suffered as a child; I did not want my children to suffer. Providing food, shelter, and care was imperative.
