About Emadeck

Made by a creator, for creators.

Emadeck exists to give your time back, so you can spend it creating instead of editing.

Where the idea came from.

I am a creator, and I kept buying templates.

Every template looked perfect in the preview. Then the real work began. I changed the colors, one page at a time. I changed the fonts. I fixed the spacing, rewrote the text, moved things around, and started again with the next page.

I was spending hours editing instead of creating the product I wanted to sell. And I kept paying for more templates, hoping the next one would be easier.

It did not need to be that hard. So I built the tool I was looking for: ready-made pages that look professional, a book you build by simply placing them, and one click to change every color and font across all your pages.

Emadeck is that tool. It started with ebooks, and it grows with planners, coloring pages, quotes, mockups, labels, CVs and cards. Everything built around one idea: make your life easier.
